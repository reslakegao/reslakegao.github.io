S / N · Information

（Sensing vs Intuition）

What is S / N？

S（Sensing）：Pay more attention to observable facts, details and what is happening at present. I like specific data, step-by-step explanations and verifiable evidence.

N（Intuition）：Pay more attention to patterns, possibilities and underlying meanings. I like to conceptualize, associate and understand things from a big picture.

Both are effective ways to obtain information. You use S and N every day, but more accustomed to using which one first

A simple comparison

  • learning kit：S likes examples and steps; N likes principles and frameworks.
  • means of expression：S is more "literal" and methodical; Be more "metaphorical", jump to the conclusion and then fill in the details.
  • attention：S will be uneasy about imprecise descriptions. N will get tired of content that only provides details and has no "meaning".
  • Preference task: S conducts processes, tests, and verifications; N Conceives, reorganizes, and identifies trends.

Study/life tips

  • To S：When doing exercises, casually write down "What does this knowledge point do in the whole?" Draw a super-simple large picture after each lesson.
  • To N：Turn your ideas into a list; Write "How to verify next and what indicators to use" for each point.
  • Team works：S controls quality and milestones; N is responsible for creativity and direction setting, and complementarity is more efficient.

Totally

S/N is not about the level of proficiency, but rather about "how one is more accustomed to obtaining information
