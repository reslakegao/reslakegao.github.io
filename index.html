What is MBTI?

Energy (Extraversion / Introversion)

Where you focus your energy: outward interaction or inner reflection

Information (Sensing / Intuition)

How you take in information: details & facts or patterns & possibilities.

Decisions (Thinking / Feeling)

How you decide: logic & principles or values & harmony.

Lifestyle (Judging / Perceiving)

How you like to live: structured & decided or flexible & adaptable.

Important note

MBTI describes preferences, not ability or character quality. Use results for self-reflection, not limits.
