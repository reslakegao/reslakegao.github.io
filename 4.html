J / P · Lifestyle

（Judging vs Perceiving）

What is J / P？

J（Judging）：like planning, making lists and making decisions in advance. Having clear milestones makes me feel more at ease.

P（Perceiving）：like flexibility, exploration and leaving room for maneuver. Rolling adjustments make me more energetic.

Both are normal. The key lies in the default organizational method the difference lies in whether to settle down first and then act, or to explore first and then converge.

Common manifestations

Learning/project habits J P

J · points

  • Make plans and set deadlines
  • Complete the controllable parts first and then expand
  • Prefer fixed processes and version nodes

P · points

  • Explore multiple options and methods
  • Allow for rolling updates of the plan
  • Prefers quick trial and error and inspiration
Risk and Balance J P

J · May be biased

  • Overemphasizing acting according to plans
  • Intolerant to changes
  • It is easy to "stop when it's done" and neglect optimization

P · May be biased

  • Continuous exploration leads to procrastination
  • scope creep
  • The decision-making window missed the best opportunity
