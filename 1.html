E / I · Energy

（Extraversion vs Introversion）

What is E / I？

E（Extraversion）：Attention is more likely to be directed towards the outside world (people, activities, interactions). Many times, it's easier to "recharge" by thinking while doing things, chatting with people or participating in activities.

I（Introversion）：Attention is more likely to be directed towards the inner world (thinking, ideas, quietness). Usually, think it through before taking action. Being alone or doing your own things can help you regain your energy.

This is not a judgment of good or bad personality, nor is it a label of "socially awkward or socially sensitive". Both E and I are normal, and everyone uses both, but which state are you more accustomed to starting from

A simple comparison

  • After school/work：E might want to go for a walk with classmates/friends. I would rather have some quiet time first before socializing.
  • encounter a problem：The habit for E is to speak out first and think about it together. I is used to thinking it over in my mind first.
  • Classroom/Meeting：E is likely to raise his hand first to speak. I often wait until I think it through before sharing.
  • Weekend arrangements：feel energetic when the E arrangement is full. I feel tired when my schedule is too full.

Common misunderstandings

  • Misunderstanding 1： "Outgoing = talkative, introverted = shy." " In fact, E/I refers to the direction of energy, not social skills.
  • Misunderstanding 2： Introverts don't like people. In fact, I also value people and relationships, but I just need some alone time to recharge.
  • Misunderstanding 3： "Never change in a lifetime." " In fact, context can affect performance, such as subjects, roles, cultural environments, etc.
